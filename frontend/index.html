📖 UNO SHOW 'EM NO MERCY RULES

💀 The 25-Card Mercy Rule

If any player accumulates 25 or more cards in their hand at any time, they are immediately knocked out / eliminated! The last surviving player wins!

🔥 Penalty Stacking (+2, +4, +6, +10)

When a draw card is played against you, you can pass the penalty along by playing an equal or higher draw card (e.g. +4 on +2; +6 on +4; +10 on +6). If you cannot match or exceed it, you must draw the full accumulated stack!

🔄 0 Cards — Pass Hands

Playing a 0 forces every single player to pass their entire hand to the next player in the current direction of play!

🤝 7 Cards — Swap Hands

Playing a 7 allows the player to swap their entire hand with any chosen opponent!

🗑️ Discard All

Playing a Discard All card lets you discard all cards in your hand that match that color!

⚡ Skip Everyone

Skips all other players — you get another turn immediately!

🎰 Wild Color Roulette

Next player must draw cards one-by-one until they hit a card of the chosen roulette color!
